### Overall Impression

The 9×9 Sudoku core and assistive-tech foundations (`aria-label`s, live announcements, reduced-motion/forced-colors) are solid, but the surrounding shell is an inherited Tic-Tac-Toe wrapper that creates high cognitive load (6/8 checklist failures) and hides the co-op/live experience that makes Sudoku Live distinct. The single biggest opportunity is reclaiming vertical focus above the board while making co-op partner presence visible directly on the grid.

### What's Working

1. **Legible 9×9 Grid & Highlighting (`src/components/SudokuBoard.jsx`, `src/styles/app.css:494-593`)**: `aspect-ratio: 1 / 1`, `clamp()` sizing, `2px` 3×3 box dividers, and distinct peer/same-digit/given/conflict states make the board immediately scannable.
2. **Strong Assistive-Tech Foundations (`src/utils/aria-helpers.js`, `src/styles/app.css:924-942`)**: Per-cell descriptive `aria-label`s, `aria-invalid`, `aria-live="polite"` announcements, `prefers-reduced-motion`, and `forced-colors` support.
3. **Remaining-Digit Counters (`src/components/PlayerControls.jsx:61-78`)**: Showing `9 - counts[d]` under each numpad button gives useful progress telemetry without cluttering the grid.

### Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Destructive mid-game resets with no confirmation or Solo persistence**
   - **What**: `switchMode` (`src/App.jsx:836-870`), Solo `DifficultySelector` `onChange` (`src/App.jsx:1017-1019`), and `handleNewPuzzleAction` (`src/App.jsx:883-914`) immediately discard an in-progress puzzle without confirmation.
   - **Why it matters**: One stray tap on mobile erases 5–30 minutes of solving progress.
   - **Fix**: Guard mode/difficulty/new-puzzle resets when `gameState.history.length > 0 && gameState.status !== 'solved'` with an inline or modal confirmation (Esc-dismissible), and persist Solo state in `localStorage`.
   - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

2. **[P1] Vertical chrome bloat, duplicate telemetry, and 81-tab-stop grid**
   - **What**: Up to 5 stacked chrome blocks push `PlayerControls` down on mobile; `filledCount/81` is duplicated in `#game-status-text` and `#sudoku-progress` (`src/components/StatusBanner.jsx:22,131`); and all 81 `.sudoku-cell` buttons in `SudokuBoard.jsx` sit in the linear `Tab` order.
   - **Why it matters**: Causes 6/8 cognitive load failures, cramped mobile ergonomics, and 81 `Tab` presses for keyboard users to reach the numpad.
   - **Fix**: Collapse `OnlineInviteBox` into a compact pill/drawer once `partnerOnline` is true; fold `DifficultySelector` into a compact header/new-game control; remove duplicate `filledCount/81` from `#game-status-text`; and add roving `tabIndex` (`0` on selected/first cell, `-1` on others) in `SudokuBoard.jsx`.
   - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

3. **[P1] Invisible co-op partner presence & silent no-ops on invalid actions**
   - **What**: `SudokuBoard.jsx` renders all non-given digits identically (`--color-user`) with no partner attribution or cursor, and `applyPlayerDigit` (`src/App.jsx:682-690`) silently exits when `selectedIndex === null`.
   - **Why it matters**: Undermines the app's co-op differentiator and makes digit-first taps feel broken.
   - **Fix**: Track `placedBy` / partner's active cell in co-op sync events and style partner cells/cursor distinctly; show a brief visible status prompt when tapping a digit with no cell selected or tapping a given cell.
   - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

4. **[P2] Flat solve-state ending & outdated Tic-Tac-Toe visual identity/docs**
   - **What**: Solving the puzzle only updates a pill string (`Solved! (Medium)` in `StatusBanner.jsx:25-29`), and `docs/images/screenshot-gameplay.png` + `docs/images/screenshot-mobile.png` still depict the old Tic-Tac-Toe UI.
   - **Why it matters**: Weak Peak-End payoff after finishing a puzzle, and documentation screenshots misrepresent the product.
   - **Fix**: Add a celebratory grid completion state + concise solve summary (time, difficulty, Play Again CTA), and replace `docs/images/screenshot-*.png` with current Sudoku Live captures.
   - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable delight`
